Pendulum: Rope Swing

Hold to grab the rope, let go to fly. Timing is everything.

A one-thumb momentum game: your release timing is graded EARLY, PERFECT or LATE, and momentum carries between swings. A hundred hand-made levels in ten painted worlds, hidden crystals on riskier routes, and one daily run that everyone plays the same day. Free to start, one purchase for the rest, no ads.

Try the web prototype Get the iPhone version early

The browser prototype is an early version; the iPhone game has 100 levels, ten worlds and the daily run.

What you do

  • Every level is beatableA simulator plays each level thousands of times before it ships; unwinnable or trivial levels never make it in.
  • Ten painted worldsCanyon, mountains, caverns, forest, desert, ocean, volcano, glacier, storm, cosmos, each with its own music.
  • Daily runOne level seeded from the date, identical for every player, with a Game Center leaderboard.
  • Six skinsThree free, three with the one-time unlock that also opens worlds 4–10.
