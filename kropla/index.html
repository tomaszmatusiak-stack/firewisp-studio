Kropla

Drink more water without thinking about it.

One tap logs a glass. Kropla works out your daily goal from your weight and height, shows progress on a home-screen widget and nudges you when you forget. Nothing leaves your phone: no account, no cloud, no analytics.

Download on the App Store Android test: join the beta

Screens

What you do

  • Goal from your body, not a guessWeight and height set the daily amount; you can override it any time.
  • Widget and remindersProgress on the home screen and gentle reminders only when you're behind.
  • Seven-day history and streaksSee the week at a glance and keep the streak going.
  • Private by defaultData stays on the device. Kropla PRO is a one-time purchase for extras, nothing is locked behind a subscription.
