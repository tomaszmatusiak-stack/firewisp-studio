Sushi Rush: Merge Kitchen

Merge dishes, keep hungry customers happy and open sushi bars across Japan.

Drag two matching dishes together to make a better one, serve customers before they lose patience, and spend what you earn on new restaurants from Tokyo to Osaka. 76 levels in three worlds, combos and boosters. No ads, no energy timers. In the browser your progress is saved on your device.

Play now in the browser Join the beta for phone builds

Android and iPhone test builds go to the beta list first.

Screens

What you do

  • Merge on a free boardDishes sit anywhere on the counter; two of a kind become one better dish. No grid, no rows to clear.
  • Customers with patienceEach order has a timer and a mood. Serve in time for tips and combos; let it run out and the customer leaves.
  • Three worlds, 76 levelsTokyo, Kyoto and Osaka, each with its own decor, dishes and generators. Coins open new restaurants on the map.
  • Fair by designNo ads and no energy. Optional gem packs exist, but every level can be finished without them.
