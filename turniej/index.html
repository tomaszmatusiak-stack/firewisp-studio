Turniej

Type the players, pick round-robin or knockout, draw the pairs. The table keeps itself.

Made for football evenings on the console or the table-football league at work: every player against every player, or a knockout bracket with byes. Enter results, the standings and the next matches update themselves, and a stopwatch is built in. Everything stays on the device.

Try it in the browser Android test: join the beta

Screens

What you do

  • Two formatsRound-robin with a full table, or a knockout bracket that handles odd player counts with byes.
  • Results in, table outPoints, goal difference and head-to-head are sorted the football way.
  • Built-in stopwatchMatch halves of any length, right next to the score.
  • No backendNothing is sent anywhere; tournaments live on the device and can be exported.
