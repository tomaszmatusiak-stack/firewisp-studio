Whirlpick

Can't decide? Add your options, spin the wheel and let chance choose for you.

Who does the dishes, what's for dinner, who goes first. Type options or paste a whole list, spin by button or by flick, and watch the wheel stop. Elimination mode removes the winner so you can draw an order without repeats. No account, no ads, no internet needed.

Try it in the browser Android test: join the beta

Screens

What you do

  • Elimination modeThe drawn segment disappears, so four players get a full order in four spins.
  • Paste a listOne option per line, up to 20 segments, emoji included.
  • Presets and themesYes/no, numbers, dinner ideas, player order; three hand-designed looks.
  • Spin by flickA harder flick spins longer, with sound on every segment and a fanfare at the end.
